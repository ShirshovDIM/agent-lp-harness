## Data window

## Method

Reference position (size omitted), entry at center, width ±2/5/10/15/30%. CL accounting from L
and bounds. Fees: the position's share of hourly ΔfeeGrowthGlobal0/1 while in range (proportional
to L; unit-tested: L equal to the pool's L earns exactly the pool's fees).
Re-range costs: the tier fee and the curve slippage apply to the **swapped volume V = φ·W**
(not the full position): a re-range exits into one asset and trades only the share φ the new
centered range does not hold. Slippage = **k/(1+k)** with k = V·(1−f)/(L_h·√P_h) — a
single-tick-range approximation on hourly liquidity (auditor spec §2, 2026-10-12; bit-exact
tick-walk `scripts/v3math.py` cross-checked on QuoterV2 quotes in `tests/`). The position's own
L is subtracted from pool L for its self-quotes. + gas $0.014 per re-range (measured from NFPM
txs). Base: 113 random starts (seed 113), 14-day horizon; n_eff ≈ 2.3 independent
windows (overlapping starts — see caveats). Results vs 100% USDm; HODL 50/50 benchmark included.

| Mode | On exit from range |
|---|---|
| hold | nothing: the range stays as entered |
| rerange | close at market, rebalance, re-enter centered at the same width |

## What the numbers say (facts, not advice)

- **Width dominates.** hold ±30% is the best median (+4.62% per 14 days) with zero P(<−5%) tail
  in this window; narrowing the window monotonically reduces income (±2%: +0.77% — the position
  is out of range most of the time and holds one asset).
- **Re-ranging with costs priced on the swapped volume.** At narrow widths re-ranging still
  destroys value (±2%: −89.29% median over 14 days — dozens of re-entries, each paying fee +
  slippage + gas on a thin pool). At ±30% re-range turns positive (+2.82%) but stays below hold
  (+4.62%): the swap costs outweigh the benefit of staying centered.
- The pre-correction formula overstated costs ~2.5–10× (wrong price power and full-W base); the
  corrected medians moved up at wide widths (±15%: −19.70% → −1.76%; ±30%: −0.06% → +2.82%).
  Narrow-width conclusions are unchanged.
- Same pattern as the Base measurements (WETH-USDC, USDC-AERO): **grid width and geometry
  decide; the swap decision is second-order.**

## Caveats

- **Market regime.** CELO rose 28.1% over the window; no falling-regime starts with exits from
  wide windows. For hold this biases income upward (rising asset in the position); no conclusions
  about re-ranging in a fall are drawn.
- **Fees via feeGrowthGlobal** without tick-outside terms (hourly global data): exact for
  positions whose bounds are always below/above the active tick; an approximation for
  boundary hours.
- **Position share of active liquidity.** A reference position at the pilot's scale equals
  ~9.7% of the pool's active L: the model does NOT account for the position's own price/depth
  impact on re-entries at that scale — not negligible; treat re-range figures at wide windows
  as conservative-to-optimistic bounds, and the calibration block (live journal) as the
  ground truth that anchors both.
- **Overlapping starts**: n_eff ≈ 2.2 independent windows (ESS ≈ 4–9 on tail probabilities);
  medians are directional, and tail probabilities carry near-zero Wilson lower bounds at this
  sample size — stated rather than hidden.
- Gas per re-range $0.014 measured from NFPM transactions (range $0.01–0.018).
- **Self-impact**: model ignores the shift of pool liquidity caused by the position itself
  (material at ~10% of active L, see above).
